Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated 12 November 2026

The short version. CricInn is a cricket scoring app. To use it you sign in with your phone number, and we keep the cricket you record — your profile, your teams, your matches and your scorecards. We do not sell your data and we do not run ads. Your phone number is never shown to other players unless you switch that on yourself. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

This policy explains what personal data CricInn collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and what you can ask us to do with it. It applies to the CricInn mobile app and to this website.

Who is responsible for your data

CricInn is operated by Gagan Choudhary, an individual developer based in India. For questions about this policy, to exercise any of the rights below, or to complain about how your data is handled, write to:

support@cricin.app

We aim to reply to every privacy request within 7 days, and to complete it within 30 days.

Everything below is listed item by item. We do not collect anything that is not on this list.

Your player profile — all of it optional except your name

You choose what to fill in here, and you can edit or clear any of it at any time: playing role, whether you keep wicket, batting style, bowling style, jersey number, city, state, country, a short bio, date of birth, gender, favourite team, favourite cricketer, favourite shot, a profile photo, and a cover photo.

Date of birth is used to confirm you meet the minimum age for CricInn and, if you choose to share it, to show your age on your profile. City and state are used to place you in local rankings and local search; leave them blank and you simply will not appear there.

Photos you upload

Your profile photo, your cover photo, and anything you add to your gallery. The app asks for permission to your photo library or camera only when you tap to add an image, and only the images you pick are uploaded.

Cricket you record

Teams you create or join, players you follow and who follow you, match fixtures and challenges, tournaments and series, and the full record of any match you or anyone else scores that you took part in — the scorecard, every ball, the runs, wickets, extras, shot types, and the point on the field where each ball was hit.

Match data is shared by nature. A cricket match has two teams. Once a match you played in has been scored, that scorecard belongs to every player in it, not only to you — so it stays visible to them, and in public records such as a team's match list and a player's statistics, even after you delete your account. When you delete your account your name is removed from those records and replaced with a neutral label. See Delete your account.

What we do not collect

  • No payment details. CricInn is free and takes no payments.
  • No contacts, call logs or SMS from your phone.
  • No background location. We only know the city and state you type in yourself.
  • No advertising identifiers. We do not run ads and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Nobody else

We do not sell your personal data, we do not rent it, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We will disclose data to a government authority or a court only where we are legally required to, and only what is required.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • See your data — most of it is on your profile in the app. Ask us and we will send you the rest.
  • Correct it — edit your profile in the app, or ask us.
  • Delete your account and data — from inside the app, or by request. See Delete your account.
  • Withdraw your consent — as easily as you gave it. Turn off notifications or number sharing in the app's settings; withdraw entirely by deleting your account.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you are unable to. Write to us and we will record it.
  • Complain — write to us first at the address above. If we do not resolve it, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To make any of these requests, email support@cricin.app from the email on your account, or include the phone number you signed up with so we can find your account.

How we protect your data

  • Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Your login code is stored only as an irreversible hash, and is excluded from every query that does not explicitly ask for it. It is never sent back to any app or browser.
  • Login attempts are rate-limited per number, per hour and per day, so a stranger cannot guess their way into your account or run up your SMS.
  • Access to the production database is limited to the developer named above.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will tell you without delay through the app or your registered contact details, and report it to the Data Protection Board of India as required.

Age

CricInn is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will raise the version number, update the date at the top, and ask you to accept the new version the next time you open the app. The current version always lives at this address.
