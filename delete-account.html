Delete your CricInn account and data

This page is for CricInn, the cricket scoring app by Gagan Choudhary · Last updated 12 November 2026

There are two ways to delete your account. You do not need to reinstall the app or sign in again to use the second one.

Option 1 — from inside the app

This is immediate and needs nothing from us.

  1. Open CricInn and go to your own profile.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, near the bottom.
  4. Read what will be removed, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — request it by email

Use this if you no longer have the app installed, cannot sign in, or would rather we did it.

  1. Email us at the address below.
  2. Put Delete my CricInn account in the subject line.
  3. In the message, give us the phone number you signed up with (with country code) and your name as it appears on your CricInn profile, so we can find the right account.

support@cricin.app

We will reply within 7 days to confirm we have found your account, and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm the request from the email or phone number on the account, so that nobody can delete your account on your behalf.

What gets deleted

  • Your account — phone number, email address, name, and your login records
  • Your whole player profile — playing role, batting and bowling style, jersey number, city, state, country, bio, date of birth, gender, favourites
  • Your profile photo, your cover photo, and every photo in your gallery
  • Your push notification token, so notifications stop
  • Your team memberships, your follows, and your followers

What stays, and why

The scorecards of matches you played in are not deleted, because they are not only yours. A match has two teams, and that record is part of every other player's cricket history — their runs, their wickets, their averages. Deleting it would quietly rewrite their records too.

What we do instead: your name and your photo are removed from those records and replaced with a neutral label, so the match still adds up but is no longer linked to you or to any way of identifying you.

We may also keep, for as long as the law requires and no longer:

  • Server and security logs, for up to 12 months
  • Anything we are legally obliged to retain, or that we need to resolve an open dispute or investigate fraud

If you want these details confirmed for your own account, ask us in the same email and we will tell you exactly what was removed and what was kept.

Before you delete

Deletion is permanent — there is no undo and no grace period, and we cannot restore an account afterwards. If you only want to take a break, you can instead turn off notifications in the app's settings and simply stop using it; your record stays as it is.

If you want a copy of your data first, email us at the address above before you delete, and we will send you what we hold.
