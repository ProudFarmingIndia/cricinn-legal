In plain words. CricInn is a free app for scoring cricket and keeping your record. Use it honestly: score real matches, upload only photos you have the right to upload, and treat other players the way you would at the ground. Scores are what people typed in, so they can be wrong. We can suspend an account that fabricates matches or abuses other people.

These terms are an agreement between you and Gagan Choudhary, an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"), covering your use of the CricInn mobile app and this website. By creating an account you accept them.

1. Who can use CricInn

  • You must be 18 or over.
  • You need a working phone number to receive a login code.
  • One account per person. Do not create accounts for other people, or accounts that pretend to be someone else.
  • Keep your phone and your login code to yourself. Anything done from your account is treated as done by you.

2. Your account and your profile

You own what you put on your profile and you can change or remove it at any time. You are responsible for it being accurate — your name in particular, because it appears on scorecards other players rely on.

Your name, photo, profile details, statistics and match history are visible to anyone using CricInn. Your phone number is not, unless you turn on sharing with your squads yourself. Your email address is never shown to other users.

3. Scoring, and what a score is worth

CricInn records what people type. It does not umpire. A scorecard, a statistic, a ranking or a wagon wheel in CricInn is the work of whoever scored that match. We do not verify matches, we are not present at the ground, and we do not decide whether a delivery was a wide or whether a catch was clean. If a score is wrong, the people who played that match are the ones who can put it right.

So, when you score:

  • Score matches that actually happened, with the players who actually played.
  • Do not invent matches, players, runs or wickets — whether to improve your own record, somebody else's, or a team's.
  • Do not score the same match twice.
  • Fix an honest mistake as soon as you notice it.

We may correct, hide or delete a match, a statistic or a ranking that we have reason to believe was fabricated or duplicated, and we may suspend or remove accounts that do it.

6. Reporting, and what we do about it

To report something urgent, write to support@cricin.app

7. Match records are shared

A match has two teams and many players. Once a match you took part in has been scored, that record forms part of every participant's cricket history — so it stays in CricInn even if you leave, and even if you delete your account. What we remove on deletion is your identity from it, not the match itself. See Delete your account.
